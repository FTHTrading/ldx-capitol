Rail · Qualified Custody

BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A.

Every deal settles through segregated vaults at a federally chartered, OCC-regulated national trust bank — requiring 2 of 3 keys so no single party can move funds, with policy-enforced controls: whitelisted destinations, dual approvals, velocity limits, and $250M in custodial insurance.

Rail · Live Reporting

On-Chain Transparency

Investors see pool NAV, collections, and collateral coverage on a live shared ledger — not a quarterly statement. Every credit decision, draw, and distribution is cryptographically receipted to an append-only audit trail. Automated covenant monitoring traps cash the moment a trigger breaches.
